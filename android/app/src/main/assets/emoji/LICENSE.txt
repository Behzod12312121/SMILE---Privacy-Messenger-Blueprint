The emoji in this directory are derived from Microsoft's Fluent Emoji, animated
collection: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji-animated

They were converted from the animated PNG the project publishes into animated
WebP, resized to 128x128, and had frames that repeat a held pose removed with
their duration folded into the frame before them. The artwork is unchanged.
